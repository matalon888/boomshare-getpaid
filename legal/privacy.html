1 · Two different roles we play

This matters, because it changes who you ask about what.

  • For freelancers who hold a BoomShare account, we are the controller. We decide what account data we collect and why.
  • For the clients a freelancer delivers to, and for the files being delivered, we are a processor acting on that freelancer's instructions. They are the controller. If you are a client and you want your data deleted or corrected, ask the freelancer who sent you the link; if you ask us, we will pass it on and help them act on it.

2 · What we collect

If you are a freelancer

  • Account: name, email, profile photo, the language and currency you choose. If you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email and account identifier — nothing else, and we do not read your calendar, contacts or mail.
  • Payments: your Stripe account identifier, payout status and transaction records. We never see or store your bank details or card numbers — Stripe holds those.
  • Your deliveries: the files you upload, previews generated from them, prices, notes, version history, and the client names you type.
  • Use of the product: pages viewed, features used, device and browser, IP address, approximate location from that IP, and error logs.

If you are a client opening a delivery

  • Your name as the freelancer entered it, and the phone number or email they used to send you the link.
  • That you opened the page, and when; whether you played the walkthrough; whether you paid or asked for changes; and any note you write back.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser, and approximate location from that IP.
  • Not your payment details. Those go straight to Stripe.

4 · Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it with the processors we need to run the service — payments, storage, messaging, email, analytics and error monitoring. The current list, and what each one does, is on the subprocessors page, which we keep up to date.

We also disclose data where the law requires it, and to a buyer if the business is ever sold — in which case this notice continues to apply until you are told otherwise.

5 · Where your data goes

We operate in Europe and serve users in Brazil and elsewhere. Some processors are based outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where data leaves the EEA we rely on an adequacy decision where one exists, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses together with additional safeguards. You can ask us for a copy of the mechanism used for a given processor.

7 · Your rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to privacy@boomshare.ai. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to your data protection authority — in Portugal the CNPD, in Spain the AEPD.

We do not make decisions producing legal effects about you by automated means alone. Reminder text may be drafted by an automated language model, but nothing is sent until the freelancer approves it.

8 · Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, Lei nº 13.709/2018 gives you equivalent rights: confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion, portability, information about who we share data with, and the right to withdraw consent. Use the same address above, and you may also complain to the ANPD. Our contact for LGPD matters is privacy@boomshare.ai; a named [ENCARREGADO / DPO — to be appointed] will be published here.
