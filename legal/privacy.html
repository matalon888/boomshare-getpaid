1 · Two roles we play

  • Controller — for your own account: who you are, what you record, how you use BoomShare, what you pay. You ask us about that data.
  • Processor — for the people in your recordings and for the clients you deliver to. You decide what goes in; we handle it on your instructions. If you are a client who received a delivery link and you want your data corrected or deleted, ask the freelancer who sent it. Ask us and we will pass it on and help them act.

2 · What we collect

Account

Name, email, username, profile photo, password or sign-in identifier, language and currency. If you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name, email and account identifier.

Recordings and what we make from them

Your recordings — screen, camera, microphone — and everything generated from them: transcripts, captions, summaries, action items, dubbed audio, drawings on the canvas, titles and notes. Also the dates and times of recordings and how you interact with editing features.

Sharing

Share links you create, and when someone opens one: the time, approximate location from IP, device and browser, and any comment or reaction they leave.

Deliveries and payments (BoomShare for Freelancers)

The files you upload, previews generated from them, prices, notes, version history, your Stripe account identifier and transaction records. We never see or store card numbers or bank details — Stripe holds those.

Your clients

The client name you type; the phone number or email you send the link to; whether and when they opened the page, played the walkthrough, paid or asked for changes; any note they write back; their IP address, device and browser. Not their payment details, which go straight to Stripe.

Technical and usage

Device type and model, operating system, unique device identifiers, IP address, approximate location from that IP, app and feature usage statistics, crash and error logs, and cookies as described in our cookie policy. Precise location only if you turn it on.

3 · Google account and calendar data

If you sign in with Google, you grant BoomShare access to your Google Calendar so that meeting-recording features work. We may access your calendar events and meeting details — titles, times, participants, descriptions and locations.

We use it only to: prompt you to record meetings on your calendar, send reminders before a scheduled meeting, and label and organise recordings with the relevant meeting details.

How we handle it:

  • Calendar data is processed in real time for reminders and organisation and is not permanently stored on our servers.
  • We do not share calendar information with third parties.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • It is never used to train AI or machine-learning models, by us or by anyone else.
  • Google profile data (name, email) is kept until you delete your account.
  • Google OAuth tokens expire and refresh as needed, and are revoked immediately when you delete your account.

Calendar access is currently required for Google Sign-In users. If you would rather not share it, create an account with email instead. You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account settings; if you do, you will need to switch sign-in method to keep using BoomShare.

BoomShare's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5 · AI processing

Transcription, captioning, summaries, action items, dubbing and reminder drafting are done by automated models, some run by third-party providers under contract.

  • Your content is not used to train third-party models.
  • We do not make decisions producing legal effects about you by automated means alone.
  • Reminder text is drafted for you but never sent to a client until you approve it.

6 · Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with: the processors we need to run the service (see subprocessors — payments, storage, messaging, email, analytics, error monitoring and model providers); anyone you choose to connect or share with; a buyer if the business is sold, under this policy until you are told otherwise; and where the law requires it.

7 · International transfers

We operate in Europe and the United States and serve users in Brazil and elsewhere. Where data leaves the EEA or the UK we rely on an adequacy decision where one exists, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with additional safeguards. Ask us and we will tell you which applies to a given processor.

8 · How we protect it

  • Encryption — in transit with TLS 1.3, at rest with AES-256.
  • Access control — role-based, least privilege, and multi-factor authentication required for all administrative access.
  • Data minimisation — we collect and keep the minimum the service needs.
  • Testing — regular security assessments and penetration testing.
  • Infrastructure — hosted on AWS and Cloudflare with encrypted storage, tight access policies and regular patching.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

10 · Your rights

EU, EEA and UK — GDPR

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent at any time. Write to privacy@boomshare.ai; we answer within one month. You may complain to your data protection authority — the CNPD in Portugal, the AEPD in Spain, the ICO in the UK.

California — CCPA and CPRA

You may ask what we collect and why, ask for a copy, ask us to delete or correct it, and limit use of sensitive information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Brazil — LGPD

Lei nº 13.709/2018 gives you confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion, portability, information about who we share data with, and withdrawal of consent. Same address above; you may also complain to the ANPD. A named [ENCARREGADO — to be appointed] will be published here.

11 · Children

BoomShare is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. Users aged 13 to 18 need a parent or guardian's consent. Payment features require you to be 18. If you believe a child has given us data, write to privacy@boomshare.ai and we will delete it.
