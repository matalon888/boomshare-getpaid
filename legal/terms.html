1 · Who you are contracting with

You must be at least 18 and acting in the course of a trade, business, craft or profession. BoomShare is a tool for professionals; it is not offered to consumers for personal use.

2 · What BoomShare does, and what it does not do

BoomShare gives you a link. You upload finished work, set a price, and send that link to your client. Until your client pays, the files stay on our storage and cannot be downloaded. When payment succeeds, they unlock.

We are a technology provider. We are not a party to the contract between you and your client, we do not review your work, we do not guarantee that a client will pay, and we take no position in any dispute between you and a client about the work itself. Your client is your client.

BoomShare is not an escrow service, a payment institution, an e-money issuer or a money transmitter. We never take custody of the money your client pays you. See section 3.

3 · Payments, and why we never hold your money

Payments run through Stripe. To take payment you must open and maintain a Stripe connected account and accept the Stripe Connected Account Agreement directly with Stripe. Stripe, not BoomShare, is the payment services provider.

Your client's payment is charged on your own Stripe account. Our fee is deducted from that charge at the moment it is made, as a platform application fee. There is no BoomShare balance, no withdrawal step and no payout queue, because at no point do the funds sit with us.

It follows that:

  • Refunds are yours to give. If you refund a client, you do so from your Stripe account.
  • Chargebacks are yours to bear. A disputed payment is charged back against your Stripe account, together with any fee Stripe applies. We are not able to absorb or reverse it for you.
  • Tax is yours to handle. You are responsible for invoicing your clients and for any VAT, IVA, sales tax or income tax arising on what you sell. We do not issue invoices to your clients on your behalf.

Stripe may hold, delay or refuse a payout, or close a connected account, under its own agreement with you. We have no control over those decisions.

You promise that, for everything you upload and every delivery you send:

What a client receives on payment, and on what terms they may use it, is between you and them. Set that out in your own agreement with your client; BoomShare does not do it for you.

6 · Storage, limits and the 90-day rule

FreePro
Per delivery5 GB50 GB
Kept live25 GB500 GB

Paid deliveries do not expire. Once a delivery has been paid for, we keep it available at the same link for as long as your account is open and these terms allow, so your client can download it again later.

Unpaid deliveries are archived 90 days after they are created, and we will warn you before that happens. This one rule exists to stop the service being used as free cloud storage by people who never send a paid link. Archived files are removed from live storage and may be deleted permanently.

We may also remove material we are legally required to remove, or that breaches the Acceptable Use Policy.

7 · Messages we send in your name

BoomShare can draft and send payment reminders and delivery notifications to your clients, by WhatsApp, email or push, so that they appear to come from you. By using that feature you authorise us to send those messages on your behalf, and you confirm you have the right to contact that client at that number or address.

Reminder text may be drafted with the help of an automated language model. Nothing is sent to a client without you approving it, and you remain responsible for what is sent in your name. You can turn the feature off at any time.

8 · Referrals

Where we run a referral offer, both the person referring and the person referred may receive a stated number of fee-free deliveries. Rewards have no cash value, cannot be exchanged, and are void where the accounts are controlled by the same person or created to exploit the offer. We may change or end a referral offer at any time; rewards already earned are honoured.

9 · Suspension and closing your account

You may close your account at any time. Deliveries that have already been paid for stay available to those clients for a reasonable period so that they are not deprived of work they paid for; tell us if you need that handled differently.

We may suspend or close an account that breaches these terms or the Acceptable Use Policy, that we are required to act against, or that is being used to defraud clients. Where it is safe and lawful to do so we will tell you why and give you a chance to put it right. Money already earned and settled to your Stripe account is unaffected.

Keep your own copies of your work. BoomShare is a delivery tool, not a backup service.
