8 · Getting paid by your clients

This section applies to BoomShare for Freelancers.

We are not a party to your contract with your client, we do not review your work, we do not guarantee anyone will pay, and we take no side in a dispute about the work.

BoomShare is not an escrow service, a payment institution, an e-money issuer or a money transmitter. Payments run through Stripe. You must open a Stripe connected account and accept the Stripe Connected Account Agreement directly with Stripe.

Your client's payment is charged on your own Stripe account, and our commission is deducted from that charge as a platform application fee. There is no BoomShare balance and no payout queue, because the money never sits with us. It follows that:

  • Refunds are yours to give, from your Stripe account. Where you refund a delivery in full we refund our commission on it.
  • Chargebacks are yours to bear, together with any Stripe dispute fee.
  • Invoicing and tax are yours — VAT, IVA, sales tax and income tax on what you sell.

Stripe may hold, delay or refuse a payout under its own agreement with you, and we cannot overrule that.

Clients who receive a delivery link are covered by separate terms for paying clients. They never need an account with us.

We can draft and send delivery notifications and payment reminders to your clients by WhatsApp, email or push so that they appear to come from you. By using that you authorise us to send them on your behalf and confirm you have the right to contact that person at that number or address.

Paid deliveries do not expire — they stay at the same link while your account is open, so a client can download again later. Unpaid deliveries are archived 90 days after creation, with warning.
