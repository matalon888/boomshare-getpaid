Terms for paying clients

You have been sent a BoomShare delivery link by a freelancer. You do not need an account, and you will not be asked to create one. These are the terms on which you use that page.

1 · You are buying from the freelancer, not from BoomShare

The work, the price and any agreement about how you may use the work are between you and the freelancer who sent you the link. BoomShare is only the technology that delivers it. We do not check the work, we do not set the price and we are not a party to your contract with them.

2 · How the page works

Before you pay you can watch any walkthrough the freelancer recorded, and see every file listed by name and size, usually with a watermarked or reduced-quality preview. You cannot download the files until payment succeeds. When it does, the files unlock immediately and stay available at the same link.

3 · Paying

Payment is taken by Stripe and charged to the freelancer's own Stripe account. BoomShare does not receive, hold or control your money. You are never charged a BoomShare fee — you pay the amount the freelancer set, and nothing is added at checkout.

Your card details are handled by Stripe and are never seen or stored by BoomShare. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

4 · Refunds, problems and disputes

Ask the freelancer first. They can issue a refund from their Stripe account. Refunds are at their discretion and under whatever you agreed with them; BoomShare cannot refund you, because we never held the money.

If the files do not unlock after a successful payment, that is our problem and we will fix it — write to support@boomshare.ai with the link and we will unlock it.

You keep any right you have to raise a chargeback with your card issuer.

5 · What you may do with the files

Paying unlocks the download. What rights you get in the work — to use it, publish it, modify it or resell it — come from your agreement with the freelancer, not from us. If that has not been written down, ask them.

6 · How long the link lasts

Once a delivery has been paid for we keep it available at the same link so you can download it again later. We still recommend you save your own copy. A delivery that is never paid for is archived after 90 days.

7 · Your data

To show you the page and tell the freelancer you have opened it, we process your name (as the freelancer entered it), the fact and time that the link was opened, your payment status, and basic technical data such as your IP address. For this we act on the freelancer's instructions as their processor. The freelancer is responsible for telling you how they use your data. Our privacy policy explains the rest, including how to reach us.

8 · Fair use of the page

Please do not try to bypass the lock, scrape the preview, or attack the page. If you think a delivery contains something unlawful or infringing, tell us at support@boomshare.ai — see the Acceptable Use Policy.

9 · Liability

We provide the delivery page with reasonable skill and care. We are not responsible for the quality, legality or fitness of the work itself, which is the freelancer's responsibility. Nothing here removes rights you have under mandatory consumer law in your own country.
