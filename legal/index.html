The short version

  • We never hold your money. Your client pays on your own Stripe account and our fee comes off that charge. There is no balance and no payout queue.
  • You keep every right in your work. We get only the licence needed to host it, preview it and deliver it.
  • Your client never pays us a fee, and never needs an account.
  • Paid deliveries stay live. Unpaid ones archive after 90 days, which is what stops the service becoming free cloud storage.
  • Nothing is sent to your client without you approving it, including automatic reminders.
  • Your content is never used to train third-party AI models, and Google calendar data is never used for training or advertising.
