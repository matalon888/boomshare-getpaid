Refunds, disputes and chargebacks

Version 0.1 · 17 September 2026 · BoomShare for Freelancers

Draft for review. These documents were prepared to match how the service actually works, but they have not been reviewed by a qualified lawyer and are not legal advice. Have counsel qualified in the operating company's jurisdiction, and in Portugal, Spain and Brazil, review them before launch. See open questions for the decisions still outstanding.

Two different things get refunded on BoomShare, and they work differently. This page keeps them apart.

1 · Money a client paid a freelancer

That payment was charged on the freelancer's own Stripe account. It never passed through BoomShare, so we cannot refund it.

  • Clients: ask the freelancer. They can refund you in full or in part from Stripe.
  • Freelancers: you issue the refund yourself. Where a delivery is refunded in full, we refund our platform fee on it as well. Stripe's own processing fee may not be returned — that is Stripe's policy, not ours.
  • Chargebacks: if a client disputes a payment with their bank, the amount and any Stripe dispute fee are taken from the freelancer's Stripe account. We cannot reverse that, and we do not take a second fee on it.

If a payment succeeded but the files did not unlock, that is a fault on our side. Write to support@boomshare.ai with the link and we will unlock it — a client should never be out of pocket because of our bug.

2 · Money paid to BoomShare for Pro

Pro is billed monthly or yearly and renews until cancelled. Cancel any time; you keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, and we do not pro-rate the remainder of a period as a matter of course.

If you are in the EU or the UK you have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal on a new subscription. Because Pro starts straight away at your request, that right may be reduced or lost once the service has been supplied — but in practice, if you subscribe by mistake and tell us within 14 days, we refund it. Write to support@boomshare.ai.

We also refund Pro where we billed you in error, or charged you after a valid cancellation.
