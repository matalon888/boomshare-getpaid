Acceptable Use Policy

Version 0.1 · 17 September 2026 · BoomShare for Freelancers

Draft for review. These documents were prepared to match how the service actually works, but they have not been reviewed by a qualified lawyer and are not legal advice. Have counsel qualified in the operating company's jurisdiction, and in Portugal, Spain and Brazil, review them before launch. See open questions for the decisions still outstanding.

BoomShare hosts files on your behalf and puts them behind a paywall. That makes it attractive to a small number of people who want to use it for something other than delivering their own work. This policy is what we enforce.

You may not use BoomShare to

  • deliver material you do not own or have the rights to sell;
  • distribute malware, ransomware, credential stealers, or anything designed to damage a recipient's system;
  • distribute child sexual abuse material — we report it to the authorities and close the account immediately, without notice;
  • distribute non-consensual intimate imagery, or content that harasses, threatens or incites violence;
  • sell pirated software, cracked media, stolen databases, leaked credentials or scraped personal data;
  • run a scam — taking payment for work that does not exist, or that you have no intention of delivering;
  • evade sanctions, launder money, or take payment for goods or services prohibited by Stripe's restricted businesses list;
  • use deliveries as general file storage or backup, particularly by creating deliveries that are never sent or never intended to be paid;
  • send bulk unsolicited messages to people who are not your clients;
  • probe, scrape or attempt to bypass the payment lock on any delivery page.

Reporting something

Write to abuse@boomshare.ai with the delivery link and what is wrong with it. We aim to acknowledge within one working day.

Copyright complaints

If you believe a delivery contains your copyrighted work, send us: what the work is, where it appears, your contact details, a statement that you believe the use is unauthorised, a statement that your notice is accurate, and your signature (electronic is fine). We will remove or disable access to material that appears infringing and tell the freelancer, who may send a counter-notice. Repeat infringers lose their accounts.

What we do

Depending on severity we may remove a delivery, disable a link, suspend or close an account, and where the law requires it, report to the authorities. Where it is safe and lawful we tell you first and give you a chance to respond. We do not routinely inspect the contents of deliveries; we act on reports and on automated signals for malware and known illegal material.
