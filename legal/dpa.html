Data Processing Addendum

Version 0.1 · 17 September 2026 · BoomShare for Freelancers

Draft for review. These documents were prepared to match how the service actually works, but they have not been reviewed by a qualified lawyer and are not legal advice. Have counsel qualified in the operating company's jurisdiction, and in Portugal, Spain and Brazil, review them before launch. See open questions for the decisions still outstanding.

When you deliver work through BoomShare, you decide what client data goes into it. That makes you the controller and us your processor for that data. This addendum sets out the terms required by Article 28 GDPR, and forms part of the Terms of Service. No signature is needed; it applies automatically to every account.

1 · What we process, and for whom

Subject matterDelivering your work to your clients and taking payment for it
DurationFor as long as your account is open, plus the retention periods in the privacy notice
Nature and purposeHosting, preview generation, link delivery, notification, payment status tracking
Types of dataClient name, contact details, the fact and time a link was opened, payment status, the notes they write, technical data such as IP address, and the contents of the files you upload
Data subjectsYour clients, and anyone appearing in the work you deliver

2 · Our obligations

  • We process client data only on your documented instructions, which include your use of the product.
  • Everyone with access is bound by confidentiality.
  • We keep appropriate technical and organisational measures — encryption in transit and at rest, access control, least privilege, logging and regular review.
  • We use only the subprocessors listed, under equivalent terms, and we give you notice of changes with a chance to object.
  • We help you respond to requests from data subjects, and with your obligations on security, breach notification and impact assessments.
  • We notify you without undue delay of a personal data breach affecting your client data.
  • On request at the end of the service we delete or return client data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • We make available the information needed to show compliance, and allow audits on reasonable notice, at your cost and no more than once a year unless a breach or a regulator requires otherwise.

3 · Your obligations

  • You must have a lawful basis for giving us your clients' data.
  • You must tell your clients how their data is used — your own privacy notice, not ours, governs your relationship with them.
  • You must not upload special-category data, or anyone else's personal data, without a proper basis and appropriate safeguards.

4 · International transfers

Where client data is transferred outside the EEA we rely on an adequacy decision where one exists, and otherwise on the Standard Contractual Clauses, which are incorporated here by reference with us as data importer where applicable.
