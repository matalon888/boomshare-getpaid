We use very few. Here is the whole list.

Strictly necessary

These make the service work and cannot be switched off. No consent is needed for them.

WhatWhyHow long
Session cookieKeeps you signed inSession, or up to 30 days if you choose to stay signed in

Analytics and advertising — only with your consent

These are off until you accept them, and you can change your mind at any time from the link in the footer.

WhatWhyHow long
Product analyticsWhich features get used, where people get stuckUp to 12 months
Advertising measurementWhether an ad we paid for led to a signupUp to 12 months

Because we advertise on Meta, accepting advertising cookies may mean a conversion event is shared with Meta. It contains whether a signup or payment happened, not what you delivered or to whom.

Turning them off

Use the cookie settings link in the footer, or clear and block cookies in your browser. Blocking the strictly necessary ones will stop you being able to sign in.
