Questions.

Does my client need an account?

Never. They tap the link, watch, and pay. No sign-up, no password, no app.

What file types can I send?

Anything. A locked file is a locked file. Images, video, PDFs and audio preview themselves automatically — HEIC and MOV included, because that is what a phone produces. For a ZIP, a PSD or a repo you attach an image or record a twenty-second walkthrough as the preview.

How big can a delivery be?

Free covers 5 GB a delivery and 25 GB kept live; Pro raises it to 50 GB and 500 GB. Uploads resume when the signal drops. Paid deliveries never expire — unpaid ones archive after 90 days, which is the one rule that stops the service becoming free cloud storage.

Do you hold my money?

We can't. The charge runs on your own Stripe account and our fee comes off it at that moment. No balance, no withdrawal, no fourteen-day wait.

What if they want changes first?

There's a button for it. Their note reaches you, you upload v2 to the same link, and the automatic reminders pause until it's their turn again.

Isn't locking the files aggressive?

It's the opposite — it's the version where nobody has to be asked. They see everything and pay in one tap. You removed the follow-up, not the trust.

Languages and currencies?

EN, PT-PT, PT-BR and ES at launch. EUR, BRL, USD, GBP. Pix and MB Way from day one.
